How to get your export from Instagram
  1. In the Instagram app: your profile → menu (☰) → Accounts Center.
  2. Your information and permissions → Export your information → Create export.
  3. Choose your profile, then Export to device.
  4. Customize information: clear everything except Followers and following.
  5. Date range: All time. Format: JSON (not HTML).
  6. Start export. When Instagram emails you, download the .zip to this device, then come back here and choose it.
